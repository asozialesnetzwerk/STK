For the 0.2 release a significant amount of bugfixes and performance improvement have been done, to give the first playable version of SuperTuxKart. Currently .tar.bzip2 and .zip Source packages are available, a windows binary thanks to some Cygwin awesomeness and a Linux x86 binary.
